MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 12, 2024 between Acme Corp ("Client") and TechSolutions Ltd ("Service Provider"). 1. SERVICES The Service Provider agrees to provide software development and consulting services as outlined in Exhibit A. All deliverables shall meet the specifications agreed upon in writing by both parties. 2. PAYMENT TERMS Client agrees to pay Service Provider a monthly retainer fee of $15,000 USD, due within 30 days of invoice. Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month. In the event of non-payment exceeding 60 days, Service Provider reserves the right to suspend services. 3. TERM AND TERMINATION This Agreement shall commence on January 12, 2024 and continue for a period of 24 months unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. 4. CONFIDENTIALITY Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other party's proprietary information and trade secrets. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of 5 years. 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the 3 months preceding the claim.
